Banquet Halls & Conference Venues in Hubballi

Host memorable events at Gala Vista inside Radiant Vista on Gokul Road, Hubballi. Our modern venues accommodate corporate conferences, business seminars, wedding receptions, and social celebrations. Each hall features elegant interiors, climate control, and flexible seating arrangements.

Gala Vista offers two versatile event spaces. Gala Vista I is designed for grand weddings and celebratory banquets. Gala Vista II is purpose-built for executive conferences, seminars, and corporate meetings.

Gala Vista I — Grand Banquet Hall

Gala Vista I provides a grand setting for weddings, receptions, and family milestones. The hall includes ambient chandelier lighting, a raised stage, and seamless hospitality coordination.

Gala Vista II — Conference & Event Space

Gala Vista II is tailored for business presentations, corporate training, seminars, and board meetings. It offers high-speed Wi-Fi, modern audio-visual technology, and comfortable executive seating.

Banqueting Amenities & Services

Every event at Gala Vista is supported by experienced event managers and technical staff. We offer flexible seating formats including theatre, round-table cluster, and U-shape conference styles.

  • High-definition digital projection and wide display screens
  • Acoustic sound system with wireless handheld and collar microphones
  • Customized multi-cuisine catering prepared by Brick and Brew chefs
  • Dedicated pre-function foyer and private host dressing rooms
  • Ample guest parking with convenient valet assistance
